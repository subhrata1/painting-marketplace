Terms of Service

Last updated: April 2026

1. Overview

The Brush Collective ("the Platform") is an online marketplace that connects artists who wish to sell original paintings with buyers who wish to purchase them. The Platform facilitates discovery, listing, and payment processing.

2. Platform Commission

The Platform charges a 20% commission on every painting sold through the marketplace. This fee covers payment processing, platform maintenance, marketing, customer support, and infrastructure costs. The remaining 80% is paid out directly to the artist via Stripe Connect.

3. Artist Responsibilities — Shipping

Artists are solely responsible for shipping sold paintings to buyers. This includes packaging, selecting a carrier, paying shipping costs, providing tracking information, and ensuring the painting arrives safely. The Platform does not handle, store, or ship any physical artwork.

Specifically, artists agree to:

  • Ship the painting within 7 business days of receiving the order notification.
  • Package the painting securely and appropriately to prevent damage in transit.
  • Provide a valid tracking number through the Artist Dashboard.
  • Bear all shipping and insurance costs, or include them in the listing price.
  • Handle any shipping disputes, lost packages, or damage claims with the carrier directly.

4. Buyer Rights

Buyers are entitled to:

  • Receive the painting as described in the listing.
  • Receive shipping tracking information from the artist.
  • Request a refund if the painting is not shipped within 14 business days, or if it arrives significantly different from the listing description.

5. Artist Accounts & Payouts

To sell paintings, artists must create an account and connect a Stripe Express account for payouts. Payouts are processed automatically by Stripe after a successful sale, minus the 20% platform commission. Payout timing depends on the artist's Stripe account settings (typically 2-7 business days).

6. Prohibited Content

The following may not be listed on the Platform:

  • Artwork that infringes on another artist's copyright or intellectual property.
  • Reproductions, prints, or copies marketed as originals.
  • Artwork containing illegal, obscene, or hateful content.
  • Non-painting items (sculptures, digital art, NFTs, etc.).

7. Liability

The Platform acts solely as an intermediary. We do not guarantee the quality, authenticity, or condition of any artwork listed. All transactions are between the buyer and artist. The Platform is not liable for shipping delays, damage, or disputes between parties, though we will assist in mediation when possible.

The Platform reserves the right to suspend or terminate accounts that violate these terms, fail to ship paintings within the required timeframe, or engage in fraudulent activity.
