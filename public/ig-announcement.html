How to post:

Feed Post: Download the post image → Open Instagram → New Post → Select the image → Paste the caption → Share

Story: Download the story image → Open Instagram → Your Story → Select the image → Tap the sticker icon → Add Link sticker → Enter https://thebrushcollective.com → Share

The link sticker is what makes your Story clickable — viewers tap it to go directly to your website.
