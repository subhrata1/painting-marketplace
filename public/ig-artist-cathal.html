Welcome to The Brush Collective, Cathal O Regan. Cathal is an Irish tribal artist and pyrographer who puts his own tribal style into everything he creates. His first piece "Ireland" is now live on our site — a stunning wood burned ash Hurley with Celtic and tribal art. A one-of-a-kind piece you won't find anywhere else.
